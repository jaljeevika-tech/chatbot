## Who we are

Jaljeevika ("we", "us", "our") uses FieldFlow, a data platform, to manage and report on our community development programs. This notice explains what personal information we collect about people who participate in or are affected by our programs ("you"), why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

## What we collect

Depending on which of our programs you interact with, we may collect:

- Your name, phone number, and location (village, panchayat, block, district, state)
- Demographic information (gender, social category)
- Livelihood information relevant to our programs (income, production figures, business activity, employment)
- Records of trainings, support, or resources you've received through our programs
- Photos or videos taken during program activities, where you have separately consented (see "Photos and videos" below)

We do **not** collect financial account numbers, government ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.), or health information as part of standard program registration.

## Why we collect it

We use this information to:

- Register you for and deliver the programs and services you're participating in
- Track our program's reach and impact for our own management and for donor reporting
- Contact you about program activities
- Meet legal and regulatory obligations to our donors and government bodies

## Photos and videos

We ask for your separate consent before using your photo, video, or name in any donor report, newsletter, or public material. If you are under 18, this consent must come from your parent or guardian, not from you directly. You can decline, and this will not affect your ability to participate in our programs. You can also withdraw consent already given at any time by speaking to program staff.

## Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on cloud servers operated by our technology providers. As of August 2026, our main database is hosted in Singapore, and the systems that collect new registrations run in Mumbai, India. **[TO BE DECIDED BY THE BOARD]** — whether to disclose this as-is or move primary storage to an India-based server is a decision our board has not yet made.

## Keeping your information secure

We limit access to your information to staff who need it to do their jobs, and we keep a record of who accesses or changes it. Your phone number, income information, and sub-district location are stored in encrypted form. We do not sell your information to anyone.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **See** the information we hold about you
- **Correct** information that is wrong or out of date
- **Delete** your information (we will anonymize your record rather than delete program history that also involves other beneficiaries, unless deletion is genuinely possible)
